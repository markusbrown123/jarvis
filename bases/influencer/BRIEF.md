# AI virtual influencer

## What
A made-up character with her own look, voice and storyline, run as a brand on Instagram and labelled as AI: in the bio and with the platform's AI label on her posts. Lifestyle and fashion content that stays within Instagram's rules.

## Not this
Not presented as a real person, and not adult or explicit content: neither is something JARVIS builds. Not built from a real person's face or likeness.

## Goal
A character people follow because she's good: nine posts that look like one person, a steady calendar, then brand partnerships and affiliate income.

## How it works
1. **Character bible**: name, adult age, personality, voice, interests, style, a backstory that gives posts something to say.
2. **Look**: a reference sheet and a workflow that keeps her face and body the same across images; a 20-image consistency test before anything is posted.
3. **Calendar**: three or four posts a week plus stories; the themes; a grid that reads well.
4. **Captions**: written in her voice, with the AI disclosure in place.
5. **Engagement**: replies and DMs are Markus's to approve or send.
6. **Income**: brand deals and affiliate links, then subscriptions on platforms whose rules allow AI personas (each checked on its own).

## Rules
- Always disclosed as AI. Instagram's community guidelines and its AI labelling are followed.
- No real person's likeness, no face swaps of real people or celebrities.
- No bought followers, no engagement bots.
- JARVIS never posts, comments or messages from the account; Markus owns it and does that.
- Account logins never go in a file.
