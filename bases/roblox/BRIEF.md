# Roblox games and servers for kids

## What
Roblox games and their servers made for a young audience, earning Robux (game passes, developer products, Premium payouts) that is cashed out through DevEx. Live ones so far: Turbo Traffic Town and Sky Spire, in the `traderedge` repo under `MarkusRobloxStudio`. The studio task queue (TT-… and SS-… tasks) runs on the Windows PC (KUSTOP) and the cloud workers.

## Where the work is
JARVIS's Roblox page: each game's tasks, play-test findings and what needs Markus (uploads, store shots). Per-game galleries are drawn from what the studio produced.

## Rules
- A young audience means the stricter rules: Roblox's policies for younger users, chat and safety settings, age-appropriate content.
- Monetisation doesn't lean on kids: no pressure, no tricks to make them spend, no pay-to-win. Anything random and paid follows Roblox's rules for it.
- Nothing collects personal information from players.
- Audio uploads are limited (10 a month until ID is verified): plan them, and the Needs list keeps track.
- JARVIS never publishes a game version or spends Robux without Markus.
