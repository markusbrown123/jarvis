Straight answers.

Does the assistant make things up?

No. It answers only from the facts we set up with you: your services, your area, your hours, and prices if you want them shown. When it doesn’t know, it says so and takes the visitor’s details for you.

Will it message my customers without me?

No. It talks with visitors while they’re on your site. Follow-ups are written for you and wait for your approval; nothing is emailed or texted on its own.

Which calendar does booking use?

Bookings run through Cal.com, which connects to Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar, so a booked call lands where you already look.

What does Premium cost?

It’s priced per project after a short call, because it’s set up around how you work. Every site starts with Launch, at $299.

Is everything in Launch included?

Yes: a custom design for your trade and service area, local SEO, click-to-call, hosting and SSL, and unlimited revisions before launch. You pay only once you’ve approved it.

Do visitors know they’re talking to an AI?

Yes. The chat says so, and every lead comes to you with the conversation that led to it, so you know exactly what was said.
